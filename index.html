How to Play Gem TD

🎯 The Goal

Defend the Gem Castle at the center from 50 waves of invaders. If 50 creeps reach the castle, you lose!

🗺️ Checkpoints & Mazing

Ground enemies spawn at WP 0 (Top-Left) and must run sequentially to WP 1 (Top-Right), WP 2 (Bottom-Right), WP 3 (Bottom-Left), and finally into the Gem Castle (Center).

Create a winding labyrinth of stone slates to maximize the walking distance for creeps so your towers can shoot them repeatedly!

Note: Flying creeps ignore walls and fly directly between waypoints!

💎 The 5-Gem Round Cycle

  1. Building Phase: Place 5 random gems on the grid.
  2. Choosing Phase: Select 1 Gem to keep as your permanent active tower. The other 4 gems turn into solid Rock Slates that block enemy pathing!
  3. Special Tower Combos: If the gems on your board satisfy any recipe in the Recipe Codex, you can combine them into legendary Special Towers!
  4. Duplicate Combines: 2 identical gems of the same tier combine into Tier+1; 4 identical gems combine into Tier+2.
  5. Wave Phase: Enemies spawn and your active towers automatically attack them.

⭐ Upgrades & Marketplace

  • Upgrade Chances: Spend gold to increase the probability of rolling higher tier gems (Chipped → Flawed → Regular → Flawless → Perfect).
  • Demolish Slates: Pay 15 Gold to remove unwanted rocks and adjust your maze paths.
  • Reroll: Spend 20 Gold in Choosing Phase to re-roll all 5 placed gems.
  • 🛒 Marketplace (Hotkey B): Make gold useful all game long!
    • 🔀 Relocation (40 Gold): Move or swap any active tower. Swapping with a rock slate is 100% pathing safe!
    • 💣 Tactical Path Traps: Place Spikes, Frost Sigils, Explosive Mines, Acid Tar, or Arcane Snares directly on creep paths.
    • ✨ Socketable Runes: Socket up to 3 runes per tower (+Speed, +Damage/Crit, +Range/True Strike, Chilling Slow, Venom DPS, or Midas Gold).
    • 🏰 Repair Castle (75 Gold): Recover +10 Castle Lives to survive difficult waves.

⌨️ Hotkeys

Space: Pause/Resume • 1 / 2 / 3: Game Speed (1x, 2x, 4x) • B: Marketplace Shop • C: Recipe Codex • ESC: Cancel Placement / Close Dialog
